Our Story

Wendy's Treats began with a simple love for baking and bringing people together over good food. Today, we create custom birthday cakes, cake loaves, cupcakes, and a range of savory Cameroonian favorites and local drinks — all made fresh, by hand, from our home kitchen.

Whether you're celebrating a birthday, hosting an event, or just craving something delicious, we're here to make it sweeter — one order at a time.
